Privacy Policy

Street Cricket: Tape Ball (Android package com.streetcricket.tapeball, iOS app of the same name)
Effective date: 1 November 2026 · Last updated: 1 November 2026

Street Cricket: Tape Ball ("the game", "we") is an independent game. This page explains what information the game handles, why, and the choices you have. The short version: no account, no sign-in, no email, no contacts, no precise location. The only personal data that leaves your phone is what online play and advertising need, as described below.

1. Information you provide

  • Player name. Before an online match you type a display name. It is stored on your device and shown to the friend you play with. You can change it at any time; it does not need to be your real name.
  • Team and player names, settings, progress. Your team name, the names you give your six players, trophies, skins, language and sound settings, and saved tournaments are stored only on your device. Uninstalling the game deletes them.

The game does not read your contacts, photos, microphone, or files. When you invite a friend, the game opens your messaging app with a pre-written message; sending it is entirely up to you.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and shows ads through Google AdMob (Google LLC): short interstitial ads between offline matches and optional rewarded ads you can choose to watch to unlock cosmetic items. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address (used for approximate location and fraud prevention), ad interactions and basic diagnostic data. Google's handling of this data is described in Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

  • Your choices: on Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID and opt out of ads personalisation in Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads). On iOS you can disallow tracking in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Consent: in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland the game shows Google's consent form before any personalised ad, and you can change your choice later from the game's Settings screen.
  • No ads are shown during online matches.

4. Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we will delete it.

5. Data retention and deletion

  • Everything stored on your device is deleted when you uninstall the game (or when you tap Settings → DELETE MY DATA inside the game).
  • The match relay keeps match data only in memory for the duration of the room (at most 3 hours) and writes nothing to a database.
  • Advertising data is retained by Google under Google's policies; use the controls in section 3 to limit it.

6. Security

All online traffic between the game, the relay and Google is encrypted in transit (TLS / wss://). The relay accepts only game messages and never asks for passwords or payment details.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example the EEA/UK under GDPR, or California under CCPA/CPRA) you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal data, and to object to or withdraw consent for personalised advertising. Because we hold no account data about you, most of these rights are exercised directly through the device controls in section 3; for anything else, contact us at the address below and we will respond within 30 days.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new "last updated" date. Material changes will also be mentioned inside the game.
