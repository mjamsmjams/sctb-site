Delete your data

Street Cricket: Tape Ball (Android package com.streetcricket.tapeball, iOS app of the same name) · Last updated: 29 October 2026

The game has no accounts and no sign-in. Everything it knows about you lives on your own phone, so you can delete it yourself in a few seconds, without contacting anyone.

Option 1 — delete inside the game (recommended)

  1. Open Street Cricket: Tape Ball.
  2. On the main screen tap SETTINGS.
  3. Tap DELETE MY DATA.
  4. The button changes to TAP AGAIN TO ERASE EVERYTHING — tap it once more within 4 seconds to confirm.

The game restarts with a fresh profile. The deletion is immediate and cannot be undone.

Option 2 — uninstall the game

Uninstalling the app from your phone removes exactly the same data as Option 1.

Option 3 — email us

If you cannot use the options above, or you want confirmation in writing, email mehmoodjamshed123@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my data". We reply within 30 days. Because the game keeps no account or server-side profile, there is normally nothing further for us to delete; we will tell you so, or delete anything we do find.
